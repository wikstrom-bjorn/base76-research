records rather than full text of everything. This repository exists so that claims made in
[sociotechnical-futures](https://github.com/wikstrom-bjorn/Strategic_Foresight_-_Innovation_Researcher) and

## Frameworks and instruments

- **Perceptual Legitimacy Gap (PLG)** — theory of legitimacy erosion under formal rationalization.
  Peer-reviewed (see above).
- **Cognitive Subsumption** — theory of capital and the enclosure of the deliberative ground.
  Under review (see above).
- **Field–Node–Cockpit (FNC)** — framework for locating self-reference and digital personhood across
  explanatory levels in AI systems. Preprint status (see above).
- **Self-Reference Test (SRT)** — instrument for applying FNC empirically.
- **Turn 5 Event** — a self-directed empirical study (not externally peer-reviewed) testing FNC's
  ability to detect self-referential coherence in AI systems. Framed here explicitly as internal
  research, not third-party validation.
